AirGlance Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

AirGlance is a local desktop image viewer published by RyzenWang. This policy describes the Microsoft Store version of AirGlance and explains what happens to information when you use the app.

Information AirGlance collects

AirGlance does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, telemetry, crash upload, or update check.

  • Images and folders you open are read locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to a third party by AirGlance.
  • AirGlance does not request network capabilities in its package manifest and does not make network requests.
  • There are no cookies, tracking pixels, or device identifiers used by AirGlance.

Data stored on your device

AirGlance stores viewer settings and thumbnail cache locally in the current user's application-data folder. These files stay on your device and are used only to restore your viewing preferences and speed up folder browsing. You can remove them by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the app.

Microsoft Store

If you install AirGlance from Microsoft Store, Microsoft may process information related to the Store listing, download, purchase, and Store telemetry under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. RyzenWang does not receive your images or personal files from Microsoft. Partner Center may provide aggregate acquisition and usage reports that do not identify individual users.

External links

AirGlance contains links to its GitHub project and third-party license notices. Those links open only when you click them and use your default web browser. This privacy policy does not control the privacy practices of GitHub or other linked sites.

Children

AirGlance does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no accounts, messaging, advertising, or user-generated content.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The current version will remain publicly accessible at this URL.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, open an issue at github.com/RyzenWang/AirGlance/issues. Do not attach private images or personal information to a public issue.
